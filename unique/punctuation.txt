PUNCTUATION_01 – Minimal & Clean

Use only basic sentence punctuation: periods, commas, single question marks and exclamation marks. No arrows, no ellipsis, no emojis, no fancy symbols. Keep it clean and readable.

PUNCTUATION_02 – Statement + Line-Break Question

Use standard punctuation for statements (periods, commas). If you add a question to the reader, put it on a new line and end it with a single "?". Do not attach the question with dashes or other separators.

PUNCTUATION_03 – Commas & Soft Dashes

Allow commas to chain short phrases, and occasional " – " or " — " to create light breaks inside a sentence. No double "!!" or "??", no ellipsis; the flow feels spoken but structured.

PUNCTUATION_04 – Arrow & Math-Like Flow

Enable patterns like "→", "->", "=", "%" inside a single line when describing relationships or rough math (e.g. volume → attention, X% allocation = Y outcome). Keep them rare and purposeful.

PUNCTUATION_05 – Hashtag Cluster at the End

Use mostly normal punctuation in the main sentence(s), and then finish the tweet with a short block of 2–5 hashtags. No punctuation between hashtags other than spaces.

PUNCTUATION_06 – Handle & URL Heavy

Allow "@" handles and at most one URL in the tweet. Punctuation stays simple around them (line breaks, commas, periods). No arrows or math symbols in this profile.

PUNCTUATION_07 – Structured Lines with Colons

Use ":" to introduce short labeled parts (e.g. "Upside: …", "Context: …"). Keep the rest of punctuation basic (commas, periods, single question marks), no arrows or ellipsis.

PUNCTUATION_08 – Ellipsis & Trailing Thoughts

Allow "..." or "…" at most once, to show a trailing thought or soft pause. Combine with regular punctuation; avoid multiple ellipses in one tweet.

PUNCTUATION_09 – Light Exclamation Emphasis

Permit a single "!" or "!!" in the tweet, usually once at the end of a sentence. No mix of "?!", and keep the rest of punctuation standard (periods, commas, single "?").

PUNCTUATION_10 – Quoted Phrases

Allow one short quoted fragment using "" or "". The rest of the tweet uses normal punctuation (commas, periods, question marks) without arrows, math symbols or ellipsis.

PUNCTUATION_11 – Parenthetical Asides

Allow one set of parentheses "( )" to add a side remark or clarification mid-sentence. Avoid other complex constructs (no arrows, math symbols, ellipsis).

PUNCTUATION_12 – Multi-Line List Feel

Use line breaks as the main structuring tool: short phrases or mini-sentences on separate lines. Inside each line, keep punctuation minimal (mostly commas or periods), no arrows or math expressions.
